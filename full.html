12Questions

The five that come up first.

Is Ascot Vale a bank?

Ascot Vale is a financial technology company. Banking services are provided by a partner bank, and deposits are held there — not on Ascot Vale's balance sheet. We name the bank in the account agreement, in writing, before an account opens.

Can an agent move my money?

Only inside a policy you set: a funded wallet, an allowlist of counterparties, a per-payment cap and a monthly budget, an expiry date, and a person's approval above the cap. An agent cannot open an account, change a payee, raise its own limit or sign for itself, and one action freezes it.

What does the payments API cost?

Published cost-plus: interchange + 35 bps + 15¢ on cards, 0.5% capped at $2.50 on ACH, 25¢ on an instant payout, $0 on disputes and refunds, no monthly minimum. On a $100 card charge that is $2.74 against Stripe's $3.20.

Who is it for?

Firms that do deals, and the entities a closing creates: a private-market firm, a fund's SPV, the company a transaction sets up. Never a personal or consumer account.

When can an account open?

The platform is in build now: accounts, instant payments, cards, approvals, agents and statements on seeded data, before a partner bank is named. Ask for access and you are in the first group that opens one.
