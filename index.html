Cards

The card is part of the control, not the expense.

A virtual card per person and per purpose. A monthly cap that shows how much of it is spent. One switch to freeze it. Every charge arrives with a receipt, and the receipt is filed against the deal it belongs to — so month-end is a read, not a reconstruction.

Controls

Above the threshold, a second person signs.

The threshold is set per firm and per entity. Anything above it waits for a named person, and the record says who asked, who signed and when. Below it, the payment goes — the control is a limit, not a queue for everything.

Questions

The four that come up first.

Is Ascot Vale a bank?

Ascot Vale is a financial technology company. Banking services are provided by a partner bank, and deposits are held there — not on Ascot Vale's balance sheet. We say which bank on the account agreement, in writing, before an account opens.

Who is it for?

Firms that do deals, and the entities a closing creates: a private-market firm, a fund's SPV, the company a transaction sets up. Never a personal or consumer account.

What does an account cost?

Pricing is agreed per firm, on the number of entities and the people who need access. There is no published card and no per-transaction surprise — ask for the terms.

When can an account open?

The platform is in build now: accounts, movements, cards, approvals and statements on seeded data, before a partner bank is named. Ask for access and you will be in the first group that opens one.
